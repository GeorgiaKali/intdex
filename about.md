This site publishes articles by Georgia Kali about [IntDEx](https://intdex.org).

IntDEx, the AI-native Delivery Experience framework, is a research project on AI-native software delivery. It is seeking IT professionals and organisations to help develop the framework.

The framework, news, and how to take part are published at [intdex.org](https://intdex.org). These articles sit alongside that site. They do not replace it.

The author hub is [georgiakali.github.io](https://georgiakali.github.io).
